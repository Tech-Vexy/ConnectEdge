One App. Three Social Lens Modes.

Switch your social lens instantly with a single tap. ConnectEdge adapts your discovery deck, algorithm, and posts to your current intent.

💕

Dating Mode

Romantic match discovery with private double-blind likes, ZK identity commitments, and compatibility scoring.

🤝

Friends Mode

Find local hangout buddies, coffee partners, fitness buddies, and weekend adventure squads in your area.

💼

Networking Mode

Connect with local tech builders, startup co-founders, software developers, and professional collaborators.

Engineered for Decentralized Freedom

Every feature is designed from the ground up without central control, ad trackers, or corporate servers.

⚡

P2P Pulse Feed

Broadcast micro-posts, ask questions, or invite nearby peers for coffee over decentralized libp2p gossipsub (`connectedge:posts`).

📷

Rich Media & Lightbox

Attach photo media directly to posts and chat threads. Tap any image to view in a high-resolution dark mode lightbox overlay.

🏰

Interest Hubs & Local Events

Join local P2P interest hubs (*Campus Tech Builders*, *Weekend Hikers*) or host local hangouts with live RSVP management.

🔒

End-to-End Encrypted Chat

1-on-1 direct messaging secured via `libsodium` authenticated `crypto_box` streams. Zero plaintext leaves your device.

📡

Bluetooth BLE & Mesh Radar

Discover nearby peers continuously in real-time using low-energy Bluetooth beaconing and local Wi-Fi WebSocket mesh nodes.

🎓

Zero-Knowledge Campus Badges

Prove student or alumni affiliation with university email domains via ZK Ed25519 signatures without revealing your identity.

Zero Central Database Architecture

Your keys, profiles, and messages stay on your physical phone. No central server ever sees your data.
